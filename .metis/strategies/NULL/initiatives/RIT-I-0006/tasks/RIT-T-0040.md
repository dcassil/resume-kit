## Objective

Scaffold the three transport workspace packages required by RIT-I-0006 plus their isolated dependencies: packages/cli exposing resume_kit_cli, packages/mcp exposing resume_kit_mcp, and packages/api exposing resume_kit_api. Follow packages/matching/pyproject.toml and packages/ats/pyproject.toml for Hatchling, [tool.uv.sources], src/<import_name>/py.typed, README, and package-local tests conventions. Hyphenated package directories must not contain tests/__init__.py because root pyproject.toml uses pytest --import-mode=importlib.
